# Retail Demand Forecast & Inventory Optimization

## Business Context
Mid-size retail company in Mexico with ~$500M MXN annual revenue.
No formal data team. Manual Excel reporting. No demand forecasting system.

## Problem
Stockouts and overstock are likely reducing revenue and tying up working capital.

## Objective
Build a demand forecasting and inventory optimization prototype to:
- Reduce stockouts
- Improve sell-through rate
- Increase revenue
- Improve inventory efficiency

## Approach
1. Simulate retail transactional dataset
2. Build baseline demand forecasting model
3. Estimate economic impact
4. Deliver interactive dashboard (MVP)

## Expected Business Impact
Even a 2% reduction in stockouts could recover ~10M MXN in revenue annually.
